### Observed source quality and matching

- `Destinations.csv` has 1,000 distinct IDs but five destination concepts. `tourism_statistics.csv` has 110 unique profile keys; five profiles match catalog concepts under exact normalization plus reviewed aliases (Taj Mahal/Agra, Goa Beaches/Goa, Jaipur City/Jaipur). The other 105 profiles remain valid metadata profiles, but have no verified catalog ID/popularity/rating relation.
- `destination_details.csv` has 999 rating rows on a 1–5 scale and all `DestinationID` values join the catalog. The experience service reports observed aggregates and no individual history.
- Hotel and food directories have 240 and 68 normalized unique cities respectively, 50 shared cities, 190 hotel-only cities, and 18 food-only cities. Normalization is conservative with explicit city aliases and does not use fuzzy matching. Destination `nearest_major_city` results are nearby lookups, not proof that the destination is in that city.
- `hotels.csv` has 536 missing `Alcohol` cells. There are 1,185 distinct raw hotel names and 44 duplicate rows under the service's case/punctuation-normalized name comparison. Source start/expiry dates parse as day/month/year, but their operational meaning is not documented.
- The hotel booking file's generic city-prefixed hotel labels do not match hotel directory facility names. Hotel room capacity does not reconcile to those bookings, so occupancy is unsupported.
- `Travel And Tourism.csv` has 1,000 rows, no blank cells or duplicate full rows, and costs from 11,000 to 1,639,200. There is no currency and source origin is not verified; these are not INR training targets.
- `user_visits.xlsx` has complete required values, 432 state/month rows, and no year. Its month groupings must not be interpreted as a current/future timeline.

## Current UI/API consumers

- Tourist catalog and recommendations use `GET /api/catalog/destinations` and `POST /api/ml/recommendations`. The recommendation service is content ranking, not a supervised model.
- Destination details call `/api/ml/destination-insights` and, after month selection, `/api/ml/destination-estimates`. Estimates are deterministic rules based on metadata and the year-free tourism profile.
- Tourist planner calls `POST /api/ml/trip-estimate`, which uses `trip_cost_rates.json`; it does not use the experimental model endpoint.
- Tourist CSV hotel page calls `/api/catalog/hotel-directory`; guide data comes from `/api/catalog/food`. Tourist/government operational hotel, restaurant, and transport pages call MySQL-backed endpoints and are kept separate from CSV references.
- Government tourism charts use `user_visits.xlsx` via `/api/ml/tourism` and admin-protected `/api/ml/dashboard-overview`; ratings use admin-protected `/api/ml/experience`. Admin-only `/api/ml/model-status` returns availability and limitations without evaluation metrics.
- Saved places/trip drafts remain in tourist browser `localStorage`. Map action is an OpenStreetMap search deep link, not custom coordinates or route planning.
- `Backend/app.py` contains a scheduled message-only `real_time_algorithm` placeholder; it does not scrape, fetch, or refresh data.

Run `python -m ml_services.dataset_validation` from `Backend` for a read-only check of headers, row counts, blanks, duplicates, numeric/date parsing, rating ID joins, directory overlap, and local image manifest paths. See `ML_README.md` for fresh terminal-only evaluation commands and limitations.
